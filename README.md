# Brew & Build

**Sun Devil Freebies** is a Cubbie Skill for ASU students. It finds free food on campus, freebies and food deals nearby, and student discounts that match what you care about, and can text you a short list every day at a time you pick.

## Install

Text this link to Cubbie and reply "yes" when it asks:

https://github.com/brew-and-build/brew-and-build/tree/main/skills/sun-devil-freebies

## Try it

- "free food today?"
- "any deals near Tempe campus?"
- "I'm broke and hungry"
- "text me freebies every day at 5 pm"

## How it works

1. The first time, Cubbie asks your campus, what you want to hear about and any diet needs, and remembers your answers.
2. It reads ASU's public events calendar and searches the web for free food, nearby deals and student discounts.
3. It opens each page and keeps only items that are valid now and match your interests.
4. It texts you the top three, each with a source link. If you picked a daily time, it repeats this every day.

It never buys or signs up for anything for you, and it needs no sign-in beyond what Cubbie already has.
