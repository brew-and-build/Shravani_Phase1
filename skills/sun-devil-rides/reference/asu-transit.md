# ASU ride cheat sheet

Facts checked October 2026. Times and prices change, so always open the live link before answering.

## Free

| Option | Where it goes | When | Live info |
|---|---|---|---|
| ASU intercampus shuttles | Between the Tempe, Downtown Phoenix, West Valley and Polytechnic campuses. Routes: Mercado, Polytechnic-Tempe, Tempe-Downtown Phoenix-West Valley, Tempe-West Valley Express | Not on university holidays | Tracker: https://asu-shuttles.rider.peaktransit.com/ · Info: https://cfo.asu.edu/pts-shuttles |
| Tempe Orbit buses | Free neighborhood buses around Tempe. Routes: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn | 7 days a week, most every 15 min (30 min on Sundays) | https://www.tempe.gov/government/transportation-and-sustainability/transportation/bus-light-rail-paratransit-streetcar/orbit |
| PHX Sky Train | Between Sky Harbor terminals 3 and 4 and the 44th Street light rail station | 24 hours, 7 days | https://skyharbor.com/ground-transportation/public-transportation/light-rail-connection |

The intercampus shuttles are for ASU students, faculty and staff. Check the shuttle page for any ID rule.

## Transit (Valley Metro)

- **Light rail hours:** Mon-Thu 4 am to midnight. Fri 4 am to 2 am. Sat 5 am to 2 am. Sun 5 am to 11 pm. Trains every 12 to 20 minutes.
- **Airport by light rail:** Ride to 44th St/Washington, then take the free Sky Train to terminals 3 or 4.
- **Fares:** Check the current fare on Valley Metro's site before quoting it. Fares were under review in 2026.

## ASU U-Pass

Unlimited Valley Metro light rail plus Local, Express and RAPID buses. Source: https://cfo.asu.edu/transit-passes

| Pass | Price |
|---|---|
| Academic year (Aug 16 to May 15) | $150, or $75 if bought after Jan 1 |
| Summer (May 16 to Aug 16) | $75 |

Return the academic-year pass before Dec 31 for a $75 refund.

## Promo code rules

- Official offers first, then public Reddit threads or coupon sites that need no sign-in.
- Posted in the last 30 days, with clear terms. Most referral codes are first ride only.
- At most 2 codes, each with its source link, labeled "unverified".
- Never enter a code or create an account for the student.
